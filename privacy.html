Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026.

DougTV is a private television interface used by one household on its own devices.

What DougTV does with your Google account

When a person in the household signs in to YouTube on the TV, DougTV uses YouTube API Services to show that person's YouTube home, subscriptions, playlists and history, and to play videos. The sign-in is kept only on the TV box it was made on.

What is collected

Nothing. DougTV has no server. It does not collect, store, sell or share personal information, and it sends no information to its authors or to any third party other than the requests the TV box makes directly to YouTube and Google to show and play videos.

YouTube and Google

By using YouTube inside DougTV you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can remove DougTV's access to your Google account at any time on Google's security settings page, or by signing out of YouTube on the TV.

Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue on this site's repository.
